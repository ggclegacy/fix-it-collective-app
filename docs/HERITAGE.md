## Future credentials

`src/lib/business-credentials.ts` is intentionally empty. Its categories support veteran-owned, women-owned, and Louisiana veteran-business credentials. Nothing renders until a verified record has an issuer, label, HTTPS verification link, past verification date and future expiration date. For credentials without an expiry, use a documented future review date as `expiresOn` so the claim must be rechecked. Rebuild when records change or a published credential expires; the homepage may be statically rendered.

Before adding a record, verify the certificate, exact business entity, approved designation, issuing authority, validity, and permission to use the mark. Retain evidence privately. Use an official public verification link. Store approved mark artwork locally and set `usageApproved` only after reviewing issuer rules. A valid text credential may render without a mark. Unverified, incomplete and expired records stay hidden. Do not use Marine Corps seals or unofficial certification-style badges as ownership decorations.
